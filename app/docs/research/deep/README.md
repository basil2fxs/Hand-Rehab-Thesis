# deep

One review per game, reading the load-bearing sources in full rather than their abstracts. Each file sets the game's parameters, scoring and analysis against the evidence, ranks what to change, and marks every source as read in full, abstract only or record only.

| File | Game | What changed from it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`reaction.md`](reaction.md) | Reaction, the study's first block | R1 one-sided; a pooled slope of RT on the wait; R3 with its pass rate at no practice; sensitivity rows; corrected claims. The task is unchanged. |
| [`rhythm.md`](rhythm.md) | Rhythm | Rh1 now asks that presses anticipate the beat, the mean reported as an estimate; the study chart frozen with its notes on the music's attacks; each row keeps its note's time; only blocks on measured delays feed the checks |
| [`echo.md`](echo.md) | Echo | Two silent misses end the game, not the block; the same sequences for everyone; the mean of two games leads and E1 reads as a feasibility check; one GET READY line; the life, latency and between-game numbers reworked |
| [`force_pilot.md`](force_pilot.md) | Force Pilot | The centre line drawn and followed; F1 the force-target correlation on irregular against repeating waves, not error against level; F2 a lag contrast, since 8 s of preview makes 100 to 300 ms the wrong band; F3 and F4 feasibility checks; one probe wording; probe peaks and the music track logged |
| [`chords.md`](chords.md) | Chords | At light presses the leak ratio is built mostly before the press and the quiet fingers unload, so C3 and the leak halves of C2 and W4 are exploratory beside a press-locked measure; C6 on each chord's mean onset; one GET READY line; the record carries every finger's level; musical training on the intake sheet |
| [`buzz_hunt.md`](buzz_hunt.md) | Buzz Hunt | The battery plays one 2.0 s window and three catch trials; B5 dropped; B2 exploratory, read against the chance its stimulated fingers give and counting everyone who played; false alarms pooled with an exact bound; a same-sample tie goes to the finger that buzzed; the stage card asks for speed; the motor claims corrected |
| [`pattern.md`](pattern.md) | Muscle Memory | A paper awareness check before the debrief; no sequence-recall game straight before it in either order; the 60 minute sitting's second block plays fresh probe riffs; one GET READY line; the old riff's intrusions, four sensitivity scores, each probe alone and a stratified split-half in the notebook; corrected claims |
| [`adaptive.md`](adaptive.md) | Adaptive | A late answer pressed just after the next cue is logged against its own trial, not scored as a wrong finger on the next; no press under 100 ms counts as a response; the battery's floor is the 30 BPM start; A6 a feasibility check; the steady pace over trials 21 to 40 and the hit rate there read beside the peak, which overshoots by about 10 BPM; one GET READY line; the controller's state logged every trial; corrected claims |

What each change did to the pre-registered design is logged, dated, in [`../healthy_baseline_study.txt`](../healthy_baseline_study.txt) under HOW THE DESIGN GOT HERE.
