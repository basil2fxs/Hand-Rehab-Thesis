# deep

One review per game, written in October 2026, reading the sources the game leans on in full rather than their abstracts. Each file sets the game's parameters, scoring and analysis against the evidence, ranks what to change, and marks every source as read in full, abstract only or record only. Read the one for a game before changing its numbers: most of the settings in `config/default.yaml` that look odd have their reason here. The letters and numbers (R1, Rh1, F2 and so on) are the checks named in the study design.

| File | Game | What changed from it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`reaction.md`](reaction.md) | Reaction, the study's first block | The task stayed as it was. R1 became one-sided, failing only when reaction time falls as the wait grows, the slope of reaction time on the wait is pooled, R3 reports its pass rate without practice, and sensitivity rows and corrected claims were added |
| [`rhythm.md`](rhythm.md) | Rhythm | Rh1 became the share of presses more than 150 ms late, where a reaction to the buzz would land; the mean timing error is reported with its interval rather than tested, since tapping to music shows little lead. The study's chart is frozen as a file with its notes moved onto the music's attacks, every row keeps its note's time, and only blocks run on measured delays feed the checks |
| [`echo.md`](echo.md) | Echo | Two silent misses end a game rather than the block, everyone gets the same sequences, the mean of two games is the headline and E1 reads as a feasibility check |
| [`force_pilot.md`](force_pilot.md) | Force Pilot | The centre line is drawn and followed. F1 compares the force-target correlation on irregular and repeating waves instead of error against level, and F2 became a lag contrast, since 8 s of preview made 100 to 300 ms the wrong band. F3 and F4 are feasibility checks; probe peaks and the music track are logged |
| [`chords.md`](chords.md) | Chords | At light presses most of the leak into other fingers happens before the press and the quiet fingers unload, so C3 and the leak halves of C2 and W4 became exploratory, read beside a press-locked measure. C6 uses each chord's mean onset, every finger's level is recorded and the intake sheet asks about musical training |
| [`buzz_hunt.md`](buzz_hunt.md) | Buzz Hunt | The timed sessions play one 2.0 s window with three catch trials. B5 was dropped and B2 is exploratory, read against the chance its buzzed fingers give. False alarms are pooled with an exact bound, a tie in the same sample goes to the finger that buzzed, and the motor claims were corrected |
| [`pattern.md`](pattern.md) | Muscle Memory | A paper awareness check comes before the debrief, no sequence-recall game plays straight before it, and the 60 minute sitting's second block plays fresh probe riffs. The notebook adds the old riff's intrusions, four sensitivity scores, each probe alone and a stratified split-half |
| [`adaptive.md`](adaptive.md) | Adaptive | A late answer landing just after the next cue counts for its own trial, not as a wrong finger on the next, and no press under 100 ms counts. The timed sessions start at a 30 BPM floor, A6 is a feasibility check, and the steady pace over trials 21 to 40 is read beside the peak, which overshoots by about 10 BPM. The controller's state is logged every trial |
| [`syllables.md`](syllables.md) | Syllables, the dyslexia case | Every case sitting opens with a fixed probe that never adapts, prompts or gives feedback, half its words never trained, so a reader's sittings form an A-B series. Speed and accuracy are read apart, the voice gets a listening check, and the case procedure is written down |
| [`mirror.md`](mirror.md) | Mirror, two-board rig only | Described as simultaneous two-hand training, not mirror therapy, with no training effect claimed. M1 became a rig check on the median gap and M2 is reported two-sided. Each board's samples keep their own time stamps, and the gap is reported as a median, a trimmed mean and the share within 50 ms |
| [`srt.md`](srt.md) | The lab's SRT, Lab session only | The copy matches the lab's script frame for frame. The Lab session plays it first in both orders, in the script's own grey squares, with a response byte per trial. The flash and the tone are timed against the markers with a light sensor and a microphone, and the setup screen no longer names a sequence or a recall |

What each change did to the pre-registered design is logged, dated, in [`../healthy_baseline_study.txt`](../healthy_baseline_study.txt) under HOW THE DESIGN GOT HERE.
