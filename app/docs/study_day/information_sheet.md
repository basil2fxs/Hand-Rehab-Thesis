# Finger rehabilitation device: what a healthy hand does

**Information for volunteers**

Researcher: Basil Toufexis, final-year Mechatronics and Computer
Science, Curtin University. Contact: ____________________

## What this is

I'm building a device to help people practise finger movements after
a stroke: a 3D printed frame with a small force pad under each
fingertip and a tiny vibration motor for each finger, played through
short games on a laptop. Before it can be used with anyone who has an
injury, I need to know what a healthy hand does on it. That's what
you'd be helping with.

## What you'd do

- One visit of about an hour. Nothing after that.
- Four questions about which hand you use for everyday things, your
  age and sex, and a ruler measurement of your right hand.
- About 45 minutes of short games on a laptop, all with your right
  hand resting on the frame, whether you're right- or left-handed.
  You press the pads lightly, like typing.
- A small motor sometimes buzzes a fingertip for a quarter of a
  second, like a phone vibrating.
- A short stretch early on and a three-minute break later. After the
  break, three of the games come round again.

## Is there any risk?

The only thing you might feel is some mild finger tiredness, or
boredom. The device runs off the laptop's USB port at 5 V, nothing
electrical touches your skin, and the pads are cleaned between people.
If anything is uncomfortable, say so and we'll stop or change it.

## Your choice

Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time, during any game,
without giving a reason.

## Your data

- You get a code (like P07). Your name is only on the consent form,
  which is kept separately from everything else.
- The games record your presses, timings and the forces on the pads.
  None of it identifies you.
- Your data is stored on the university's storage, not on personal
  cloud folders, and is used for my thesis and any paper that comes
  from it. Nobody is ever named.
- You can ask for your data to be removed until the analysis starts.
  Just quote your code.

## Things to know

- Some games have a hidden rule you aren't told about beforehand. I'll
  explain them at the end.
- This is a research prototype, not a medical device, and nothing in
  the session is a health assessment.

Questions? Ask now, or contact me on the details above.
