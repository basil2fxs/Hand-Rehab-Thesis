Spoken words and syllables for the Syllables mode. `<word>.wav` (or `.ogg`) is the whole word. A word's syllables are played as they sound in that word: `manifest.json`'s `syllable_map` lists, for each word, one file per syllable, `chunks/<chunk>.wav` where the chunk said on its own sounds the same as in the word and `syllables/<word>_<k>.wav` where it does not (the "ger" of tiger is "guh", not the "jer" of the spelt chunk). A word the map does not cover plays its chunks' files. `chunks/<chunk>.wav` is one written chunk said as a spelling pronunciation; `<word>_<k>.ogg` is an older per-word render, used only where neither exists. `manifest.json` also records who or what made the files, the date and each file's length and level.
The voice is synthetic: Kokoro-82M (Apache 2.0 weights), British voice bf_emma, every chunk and word in the bank, made by `scripts/syllables_tts.py`; `--syllables-only` makes the syllables that differ from their chunk files and writes the map. A missing file falls back to the macOS `say` command on a developer Mac and to silence anywhere else, logged once per item.
A recorded Australian voice can replace it: `python3 scripts/syllables_recording_kit.py list` writes the reading script, `cut` (with `--force` over the synthetic manifest) turns the recorded pages into these files and `check` lists what is still missing. The kit's docstring covers the session.
