Spoken words and syllables for the Syllables mode. `chunks/<chunk>.wav` is one written chunk, said once as a spelling pronunciation and played in every word that holds it; `<word>.wav` (or `.ogg`) is the whole word; `<word>_<k>.ogg` is an older per-word render of the k-th syllable, used only where no chunk file exists. `manifest.json` records who or what made the files, the date and each file's length and level.
The voice is synthetic: Kokoro-82M (Apache 2.0 weights), British voice bf_emma, every chunk and word in the bank, made by `scripts/syllables_tts.py` to the recording kit's spelling rules. A missing file falls back to the macOS `say` command on a developer Mac and to silence anywhere else, logged once per item.
A recorded Australian voice can replace it: `python3 scripts/syllables_recording_kit.py list` writes the reading script, `cut` (with `--force` over the synthetic manifest) turns the recorded pages into these files and `check` lists what is still missing. The kit's docstring covers the session.
