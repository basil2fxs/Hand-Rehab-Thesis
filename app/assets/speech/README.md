Spoken words and syllables for Syllables. `<word>.wav` is the whole word, `chunks/<chunk>.wav` a written chunk said as it is spelt, and `syllables/<word>_<k>.wav` a syllable said as it sounds in its word where that differs (the "ger" of tiger). `manifest.json` lists each word's files and how they were made.
The voice is synthetic: Kokoro-82M (Apache 2.0), British voice bf_emma, made by `scripts/syllables_tts.py`. A missing file is logged once and plays silence (or the Mac's own voice when run from source).
To use a recorded voice instead: `python3 scripts/syllables_recording_kit.py list` writes the reading script, `cut` turns the recordings into these files and `check` lists what is still missing.
