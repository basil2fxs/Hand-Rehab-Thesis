Tracks the menu and Rhythm mode play (`.mp3 .wav .ogg .flac`), steady tempo, mostly instrumental, under 5 MB each.
CC0 or CC-BY only, with the licence saved beside the track as `<song>.LICENCE.txt`; credits in [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md).
An empty folder means Rhythm will not start: drop a track in and press Rescan on its setup screen.
