Flashing from Settings. Two buttons in the Arduino firmware column of the Settings screen; the hexes and avrdude ship inside the app, so nothing is installed.
Flash firmware: press, confirm, wait about ten seconds. The board restarts and buzzes all four motors, which is the confirmation. The caption under the buttons names the hex the app carries (a short commit).
Sensor address: connect ONE sensor, enter the old and new address, confirm. The app flashes a small tool sketch, moves the sensor, then puts the game firmware back. Scan lists what answers on the bus.
Every SingleTact also answers 0x04, so a change from 0x04 with other sensors wired in would move all of them; the app refuses that case. The device wants 0x05 index, 0x06 middle, 0x07 ring, 0x08 little.
No Arduino found: plug it in. No port on Windows at all: install the CH340 driver, CH341SER from wch-ic.com. macOS 10.14 and later need no driver.
Did not answer at 115200 or 57600: a Nano carries one of two bootloaders and the app tries both. Check the cable (some are charge only), unplug and replug, try again.
Could not open the port: close the Arduino IDE, any serial monitor, or the other copy of the app. "The GAME firmware is NOT on the board": press Flash firmware.
This Mac needs Rosetta: run softwareupdate --install-rosetta in Terminal once, then try again.
Whatever the message says, the full avrdude output is in the app log.
Rebuild the hexes with python3 builds/build_firmware.py (PlatformIO) and fetch the uploader with python3 builds/fetch_avrdude.py; the build scripts and CI run both, and a build without them still runs and says what is missing.
