# thesis

The written work behind this repository, as submitted to Curtin University in November 2026 for MXEN4004 Mechatronic Engineering Research Project 2, without the submission letter and student details.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `Thesis.pdf` | Development and Design of a Multi-Modal Finger Exercising Device for Stroke Rehabilitation: Software, Testing in Healthy Hands Before Patient Trials, and an Extension to Parkinson's Disease and Dyslexia. Six chapters, the references and appendices A to M |
| `IEEE paper.pdf` | Verified Game Software for a Finger Rehabilitation Device with Modes for Stroke, Parkinson's Disease and Dyslexia. Eight pages in the IEEE conference format |

Chapter 3 of the thesis describes the software in this repository, Chapter 4 reports the checks made with it, and Appendix K documents the study kit in [`app/docs/study_day`](../app/docs/study_day) and [`analysis`](../analysis). The data behind Chapter 4 stay off GitHub: [`FINAL TRIAL RESULTS`](../FINAL%20TRIAL%20RESULTS).
